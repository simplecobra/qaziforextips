Break of Structure (BOS)

A BOS tells you the trend is still alive. In an uptrend the previous high breaks; in a downtrend the previous low breaks. But there is one condition most traders forget: the candle body must close beyond the level.

What is a BOS?

A BOS (Break of Structure) means structure breaks in the direction of the current trend:

  • Bullish BOS: in an uptrend the previous HH breaks. Buyers are still in control.
  • Bearish BOS: in a downtrend the previous LL breaks. Sellers are still in control.

The key condition: a body close

Count a BOS only when a candle body closes beyond the level. If only the wick went beyond and the body closed back inside, it is not a BOS. It is often a liquidity sweep, after which price may reverse.

RememberDon't decide while the candle is still forming. The body may be beyond the level now and come back inside by the close. Always wait for the close.

Bearish BOS

How to mark a BOS

  1. Draw a straight line from the swing (high or low) that broke.
  2. End the line at the candle whose body closed beyond the level.
  3. Write "BOS" on the line.
  4. Add the timeframe, for example "H1 BOS".

Why the BOS matters

  • Bias confirmation: each new BOS says the trend continues, so look for setups in that direction.
  • Finding POIs: order blocks and FVGs form at the origin of the move that made the BOS. Phase 3 uses those zones.
  • Protected low or high: after a BOS, the low where the BOS move began now protects the trend (lesson 1.5).

What happens after a BOS?

Price often pulls back after a BOS. The pullback is a trade opportunity, but not immediately: traders who jump on the first pullback are often trapped (IDM), which lesson 1.6 explains.

Common mistakeTreating a wick break as a BOS. This is the most common mistake, and it is why traders get trapped buying breakouts on sweeps.
Common mistakeTreating the break of an internal (small) swing as a major BOS. Only a major swing BOS confirms or changes bias (lesson 1.4).
Common mistakeTrusting a BOS without momentum. A BOS made with large, strong candles (displacement) is more reliable.

Wick versus body: in one chart

See BOS's most important rule in one picture:

  • Wick only: price went above the high but was not accepted there. This is often a liquidity sweep (Phase 2).
  • Body close: price closed above the high, so the higher price was accepted. This is a BOS.

Marking a BOS: step by step

  1. First decide the structure: uptrend or downtrend.
  2. In an uptrend, watch the last major swing high (HH).
  3. When a candle closes its body above that high, draw a horizontal line from the high to that candle and write "BOS".
  4. Write the timeframe next to it (for example "BOS H1"), because an M15 BOS and an H4 BOS are not the same.
  5. Now find the new HL: the low where the BOS move started is the next important low.
  6. In a downtrend do the reverse: a body close below the last LL = a bearish BOS.

Strong and weak BOS

Not every BOS is equal:

Strong BOSWeak BOS
From a big momentum candle, with an FVGFrom small candles, slowly
Closes well beyond the highCloses just beyond the high
In the direction of the higher timeframe biasAgainst the higher timeframe bias
In a killzoneIn the quiet Asian hours

After a strong BOS, its origin (order block or FVG) makes a good POI. Trust a weak BOS less.

A Gold example

Gold is in an H1 uptrend; the last HH is at 4,152.40.

  • In London a candle reaches 4,156.10 but closes at 4,150.80: not a BOS, only a wick.
  • In New York a big candle goes from 4,149 and closes at 4,161.30: a BOS (H1).
  • The new important low is where this move began, say 4,144.50. The uptrend holds as long as price doesn't close below it.

Real Gold chart examples

Gold H1 on 4 and 5 Nov 2026 (data: Dukascopy, bid price; Pakistan time):

  • The high at 18:00 on 3 Nov was 3,970.9. The 10:00 H1 candle on 4 Nov closed its body at 3,997.5, above it: the first BOS.
  • Then a high formed at 4,070.3 at 23:00 on 4 Nov. The 05:00 candle on 5 Nov closed at 4,088.3: the second BOS, and price reached 4,146.8 that morning.

In both cases the decision came from the close, not a wick.

Second example: bearish BOS (22 Oct)

Gold H1 on 22 Oct 2026 (data: Dukascopy, bid price; Pakistan time), this time to the downside:

  • The low at 00:00 was 4,104.6. The 08:00 candle closed its body at 4,092.0, below it: the first bearish BOS.
  • Then a low formed at 4,066.6 at 10:00. The first close below it was the 16:00 candle, at 4,059.4: the second BOS. The candles from 11:00 to 15:00 kept closing above that level, so they were not a BOS.

Both times the decision came from the close. A bearish BOS means the downtrend continues: look for sells, not buys.

Common mistakes

Common mistakeTreating a wick as a BOS. Acceptance is proven only by a body close.
Common mistakeChasing the BOS candle. A BOS tells you the trend is alive; the entry comes on the pullback from a POI.
Common mistakeTreating a lower timeframe BOS as equal to a higher timeframe one.

Summary

  • BOS = the previous swing broken by a body close in the trend direction.
  • Draw the line from the swing to the breaking candle, with the timeframe.
  • A strong BOS comes with momentum, an FVG and the bias.
  • After a BOS, the entry comes on the pullback from its origin.

    Questions

    What is a break of structure (BOS)?

    A candle body closing beyond the previous swing high in an uptrend, or the previous swing low in a downtrend, confirming the trend continues.

    Does a wick count as a BOS?

    No. A wick beyond the level with the body closing back inside is usually a liquidity sweep.

    Where should the BOS line end?

    At the candle whose body closed beyond the broken swing.

    What is the difference between BOS and CHoCH?

    BOS breaks structure in the direction of the trend; CHoCH is the first break against it.

    Should I enter right after a BOS?

    No. Wait for a pullback to a valid POI; the first pullback is often inducement.

    Is a BOS with momentum more reliable?

    Yes. A BOS made with displacement candles is more likely to hold than a slow, weak break.

    What is BOS and CHoCH in trading?

    BOS (break of structure) is a body close through the previous swing in the trend direction: the trend continues. CHoCH (change of character) is the first such break against the trend: the first sign of a reversal.

    Is there a BOS and CHoCH strategy?

    Yes: take the direction from a higher timeframe BOS, wait for price to reach a POI in that direction, then enter on a lower timeframe CHoCH. That is model B (lesson 5.3), with a stop loss and 1% risk.

    How do you mark a BOS on a chart?

    Draw a horizontal line from the previous swing high (uptrend) or swing low (downtrend) to the candle whose body first closed beyond it, and label it BOS there.
