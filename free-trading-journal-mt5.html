Install in 2 minutes

  1. Download the free zip and unzip it (right-click → Extract All).
  2. In MetaTrader 5 open File → Open Data Folder → MQL5 → Indicators and copy QFT_Trading_Coach.ex5 there.
  3. In the Navigator (Ctrl+N) right-click Indicators → Refresh.
  4. Drag QFT_Trading_Coach onto any chart. In the Common tab tick Allow DLL imports (only for the Save As window and opening the report) and press OK.
  5. In the Toolbox → History tab right-click → All History once, so the coach reads all your trades.

Questions

Is QFT Trading Coach really free?

Yes. QFT Trading Coach, Journal & Report is 100% free for MetaTrader 5, with no trial, no licence key, no sign-up and no subscription. It is one of the free tools of Qazi Forex Tips.

What is the best free trading journal for MT5?

The best journal is the one you actually use, so it should need no typing. QFT Trading Coach reads your MT5 history by itself, finds your mistakes, shows what they cost and gives you a weekly plan. Web journals such as TradeZella or Tradervue are powerful but paid and need imports; QFT Trading Coach is free and runs inside MetaTrader 5.

Do I have to enter my trades by hand?

No. The coach reads every closed trade from your MT5 history: entry, exit, lots, stop loss, result, swap and commission. Right-click the History tab and choose All History once so all trades are loaded.

Which trading mistakes does it find?

Nine: revenge trade (within 15 minutes of a loss), bigger lot after a loss, no stop loss, risking more than 2%, trading after the 3% daily loss limit, overtrading, holding a loser too long, closing a winner too early and trading within 15 minutes of high-impact news. Every rule can be changed in the settings.

Why does it ask for Allow DLL imports?

Only to open the Windows Save As window, so you can save the report anywhere, and to open the report by itself. It uses Windows' own shell, downloads nothing and sends nothing. Without the tick the tool still works and saves reports in MQL5 > Files > QFT Reports.

Is my trading data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated on your own computer. There is no account, no server and no internet connection involved.

Can I get the report in Urdu?

Yes. Press Urdu report for a separate report in easy Roman Urdu, or English report for English. Both have the same graphs and lesson links.

What is a trade grade?

Every trade gets A+ to F for how it was traded, not for whether it won: stop loss, correct risk and no emotional mistakes score high. A loss with a proper stop can be A+; a lucky win without a stop is F.

Does it work for prop firm traders?

Yes. The prop firm check tests your closed trades against 5% daily and 10% maximum loss rules (changeable), and the live coach warns you when your daily loss limit is reached.

Is there an MT4 version?

The MT4 version is in progress. The MT5 version is available now as a free download.

Does the trading coach place trades?

No. It is an indicator: it reads your history, draws your trades on the chart, sends warnings and makes reports. You place every trade yourself.
