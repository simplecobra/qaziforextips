Questions

Replay software questions

Try QFT Replay Simulator free
What is the best forex replay software?

It depends on where you trade. If you trade on MetaTrader 5 and want replay inside MT5 with your own broker's data, QFT Replay Simulator or Soft4FX fit best. If you want to backtest in a browser on any computer, FX Replay or Forex Tester Online. If you already use TradingView, its Bar Replay is a free place to start.

Is there a Soft4FX alternative for MT5?

Yes. QFT Replay Simulator runs inside MetaTrader 5 like Soft4FX and adds a Prop Firm Challenge mode, lot size from your $ risk and a backtest report with a money management plan. You can try it free with the demo (5 trades).

Is there an FX Replay alternative that works inside MT5?

Yes. FX Replay runs in the browser. QFT Replay Simulator gives the same candle-by-candle replay and prop firm practice inside your own MetaTrader 5, on your broker's price history.

Does MT5 have bar replay for manual trading?

Not built in. The MT5 Strategy Tester's visual mode is made for testing Expert Advisors, not for clicking Buy and Sell yourself. A replay simulator such as QFT Replay Simulator or Soft4FX adds manual bar replay to MT5.

Can I practise a prop firm challenge with replay software?

Yes. QFT Replay Simulator, FX Replay and Forex Tester Online (Pro) have a prop firm mode where you set the profit target, daily loss and maximum loss. Soft4FX has no built-in prop firm mode, so you track the rules yourself.

Is there free forex replay software?

Free options with limits: TradingView Bar Replay on daily and higher timeframes, FX Replay's free Beginner plan, and the free demos of QFT Replay Simulator, Soft4FX and Forex Tester.
