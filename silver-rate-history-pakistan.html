Why silver moves faster than gold

Silver is priced the same way as gold, from the world price in dollars and the rupee rate, but the market is much smaller and about half of the metal is used by industry (solar panels, electronics, batteries). So news about the economy, interest rates and factory demand moves it harder in both directions. How is the silver rate per tola calculated?

Price per tola = silver price in US dollars per ounce × USD/PKR ÷ 31.1035 × 11.6638. 10 gram = tola ÷ 11.6638 × 10; 1 kg = tola ÷ 11.6638 × 1,000. Sarafa rates add a local premium.

Why does silver move more than gold?

Silver is a smaller market and half of it goes into industry (solar panels, electronics), so both buying waves and sell-offs are sharper. Since 4 March 2024 silver changed +72% in rupees per tola.
