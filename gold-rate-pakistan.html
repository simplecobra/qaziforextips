How the gold rate in Pakistan is calculated

Pakistan imports almost all of its gold, so the local price follows two numbers: the international gold price in US dollars per ounce (the XAUUSD price that traders see in MetaTrader) and the dollar rate (USD/PKR). This page uses exactly that formula, so you can check every number yourself:

24K price per tola = Gold price per ounce ($) × USD/PKR ÷ 31.1035 × 11.6638
22K = 24K × 22 ÷ 24 · 21K = 24K × 21 ÷ 24 · 18K = 24K × 18 ÷ 24

That is why the gold rate in Pakistan can change even when the world gold price is flat: if the rupee gets weaker against the dollar, the same ounce of gold costs more rupees. When both move up together, the local price jumps quickly.

Market rate vs sarafa rate: why the two can differ

The rate on this page is the market rate, worked out live from the international price and the dollar. The rate you hear on the news is usually the one the sarafa associations in Karachi and other cities announce for the local bullion market. It follows the same international price, but local dealers add their own premium (or discount), depending on supply, demand and the open-market dollar. So the sarafa rate is often a little higher or lower than the market rate.

At the shop, the price of jewellery also includes making charges (mazdoori), wastage (kaat) and the jeweller's margin, and the buying rate for your old gold is lower than the selling rate. Use this page to know the fair value of the gold itself, then compare what the jeweller quotes.

Gold rate in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and other cities

The base gold rate is the same in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Hyderabad and Sialkot, because every city follows the same international price and dollar rate. Differences between cities come only from local dealer premiums, making charges and each shop's buy/sell spread, usually a few hundred to a few thousand rupees per tola.

Gold rate in Pakistan: frequently asked questions

What is the gold rate in Pakistan today?

On 13 November 2026 the 24K gold rate in Pakistan is Rs 406,007 per tola and Rs 348,092 per 10 grams, based on the international price of $3,911.58 per ounce and USD/PKR 276.79. 22K gold is Rs 372,173 per tola.

How is the gold price per tola calculated?

Gold price per tola = international gold price per ounce (USD) × USD/PKR rate ÷ 31.1035 × 11.6638. One troy ounce is 31.1035 grams and one tola is 11.6638 grams. For 22K multiply by 22/24, for 21K by 21/24 and for 18K by 18/24.

Why is the sarafa (jeweller) rate different from this rate?

This page shows the rate from the international market and the live dollar rate. The rate announced by the sarafa associations in Pakistan is set by local dealers and can be a little higher or lower, and jewellers add making charges (mazdoori) and their margin on jewellery.

Is the gold rate the same in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad?

The base rate is the same across Pakistan because it follows the international price and the dollar rate. Small differences come from local dealer premiums, making charges and the buy/sell spread of each shop.

What is the difference between 24K, 22K and 21K gold?

24K is 99.9% pure gold, mostly sold as bars and biscuits. 22K is about 91.7% gold and is the most common purity for jewellery in Pakistan. 21K is 87.5% gold and 18K is 75% gold.

How often is this gold rate updated?

Every 30 minutes while the gold market is open, Monday to Friday. On Saturday and Sunday the international market is closed, so the last Friday price stays on the page.
