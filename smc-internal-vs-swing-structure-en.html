Internal vs Swing Structure

Two kinds of structure run on the same chart: the swing structure of the big waves and the internal structure inside one wave. Mixing them up is the most common confusion in SMC. In this lesson you will learn to read them separately.

Two levels, two jobs

LevelWhat it isWhat it is for
Swing (major) structureThe big waves that make new HHs or LLsBias and trend
Internal (minor) structureThe small swings inside one big wave or pullbackEntry timing and the end of a pullback

What happens inside a pullback?

When price pulls back in an uptrend, the pullback is itself a small downtrend: it makes LHs and LLs inside it. That is internal structure. The bigger trend is still bullish because the swing HL still holds.

The rule for telling them apart

  • A high formed by a low that does not break the previous swing LL (or the swing HL in an uptrend) is internal.
  • The move that breaks a big high or low is part of swing structure.
  • Keep labels separate: HH, HL, BOS for swing structure; i-LH, i-LL, i-BOS and a lighter colour for internal structure.
RememberAsk: "If this swing breaks, does the bigger trend change?" If not, it is internal.

What internal structure is for

  1. The end of a pullback: in an uptrend pullback, when internal structure turns bullish again (an internal CHoCH up), the pullback is likely ending.
  2. Entry timing: at a higher-timeframe zone, a lower-timeframe internal CHoCH is the entry signal.
  3. Inducement: internal lows and highs are where retail enters early; these are the IDM (lesson 1.6).

A common confusion and its fix

A trader says: "H1 made a BOS down, so I sell." But that BOS was only an internal BOS inside a pullback; the bigger structure was still bullish, and price went back up from the swing HL. The fix: for every BOS and CHoCH, ask which level it belongs to. Take bias from swing structure only.

Common mistakeWriting two LHs in a row. There is always a low between two highs; if that low does not break the previous LL, the second high is internal.
Common mistakeChanging bias on an internal BOS. Bias changes only with a swing CHoCH.

Internal structure as an entry trigger

The most useful job of internal structure is this: the swing trend is up, price is pulling back down, and inside the pullback a small downtrend forms (i-LH and i-LL). When that internal downtrend breaks, an internal CHoCH, the pullback is often over and the swing trend resumes.

Step by step

  1. Decide the swing structure (for example an H1 uptrend).
  2. When a pullback starts, treat it as part of the swing trend, not a trend change.
  3. Label the small highs and lows inside the pullback with "i-": i-LH, i-LL.
  4. Wait for price to reach a POI or the discount zone.
  5. There, the pullback's last i-LH breaks with a body: an i-CHoCH.
  6. Enter at the origin of the i-CHoCH move, stop below the pullback low, target the swing high or beyond.

Writing both structures together

  • Swing: thick lines, big labels: HH, HL, BOS.
  • Internal: thin or dashed lines, small labels with "i-".
  • If it gets confusing, keep internal labels in a separate colour and show them only during pullbacks.

A Gold example

H1 uptrend, last swing high 4,158, last HL 4,131. Price is pulling back.

  • On M15 inside the pullback: 4,150 (i-LH), 4,143 (i-LL), 4,147 (i-LH), 4,138 (i-LL).
  • Price reaches an H1 FVG near 4,136, then an M15 body closes above 4,147: an i-CHoCH.
  • Buy on the pullback near 4,141, stop below 4,135, target 4,157 (just before the swing high). About 6 dollars of risk for 16 dollars of target.

Real Gold chart examples

Gold H1 from 6 to 10 Nov 2026 (data: Dukascopy, bid price; Pakistan time):

  • Swing structure: the swing low at 4,027.2 on the evening of 6 Nov and the swing high at 4,148.6 late on 10 Nov. That is the big move.
  • Internal structure: the smaller highs and lows inside it: 4,081.0, 4,054.1, 4,133.9 and 4,084.5 (blue rings).
  • The drop to the internal low at 4,084.5 on 9 Nov could look like a downtrend to a new trader, but the swing low at 4,027.2 never broke, so the bigger structure stayed bullish and price went on to 4,148.6.

Second example: bullish internal inside a bearish swing (11 Nov)

Gold H1 from 10 to 12 Nov 2026 (data: Dukascopy, bid price; Pakistan time). This is the reverse of the first example: internal structure went up while swing structure was down:

  • Swing structure: the swing high of 4,079.6 at 18:00 on 10 Nov and the swing low of 4,013.4 at 21:00.
  • Internal structure: on 11 Nov price rose and the 10:00 candle closed above the internal high at 4,052.4: an internal BOS up. Anyone watching only that would have bought.
  • But price reached only 4,066.8; the swing high at 4,079.6 never broke.
  • At 20:00 price closed below the swing low at 4,013.4: a swing BOS down, and by the morning of 12 Nov it was at 3,968.6.

Lesson: an internal BOS can be part of a pullback; the swing structure decides the trend.

Common mistakes

Common mistakeSelling an internal LL as if it were a swing CHoCH. While the swing HL (4,131) holds, the swing trend is up.
Common mistakeEntering on every i-CHoCH. Take only the ones that form at a POI or in discount.

Summary

  • Swing structure gives the bias; internal structure shows the pullback ending.
  • An i-CHoCH inside the pullback is an entry signal in the direction of the swing trend.
  • Keep the two in separate labels and styles.

    Questions

    What is internal structure in SMC?

    The smaller swings inside a larger leg or pullback; they do not change the higher-level trend.

    What is swing structure?

    The main swings that create new higher highs or lower lows; bias comes from them.

    Which structure should I take bias from?

    Only from swing (major) structure. Internal structure is for entry timing.

    How do I tell internal and swing structure apart?

    Ask whether breaking that swing would change the bigger trend. If not, it is internal.

    Can an internal BOS change my bias?

    No. Only a swing CHoCH changes bias; internal breaks are used for entry timing.

    Why does internal structure matter for entries?

    An internal CHoCH back in the trend direction often marks the end of a pullback at a POI.

    What is iBOS?

    iBOS means internal BOS: a break of a small high or low inside a bigger swing. It is often just part of a pullback and does not change the trend on its own.

    What if internal and swing structure point different ways?

    Take bias from the swing structure. Treat the internal structure as a pullback and enter when it turns back in the swing direction.
