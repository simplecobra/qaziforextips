Lesson notes

What is forex?

Forex comes from two English words: foreign and exchange. It means changing the currency of one country into the currency of another. When someone in Pakistan buys US dollars, when a company in India buys euros to import a machine from Europe, or when a tourist changes money into dirhams in Dubai, that is forex.

Forex trading means buying or selling a currency with the aim of profiting from a change in its price.

How big is the forex market?

According to the Bank for International Settlements (BIS) Triennial Survey, about $9.6 trillion worth of currencies changed hands every day in April 2025. That is why forex is called the largest financial market in the world. It has no single building: banks and institutions around the world are linked by computers, and the market runs 24 hours a day, five days a week (see the market timings in Pakistan and India).

A simple example: the money changer

Ahmed buys 100 US dollars from a money changer at Rs 280 per dollar and pays Rs 28,000. A few days later the dollar rises to Rs 285. He sells the same 100 dollars and receives Rs 28,500: a profit of Rs 500.

If the dollar had fallen to Rs 276, he would have received Rs 27,600: a loss of Rs 400. This is the whole idea behind forex trading: profit or loss from the difference in price. (The numbers are only an example.)

Why do currency prices change?

Because of demand and supply. When many people and companies want dollars and there are few dollars available, the dollar becomes expensive. When there are plenty of dollars and few buyers, it becomes cheaper. Demand is moved by interest rates, inflation, economic news and world events. Each of these gets its own lesson later in the series.

How online forex trading works

Today most forex trading happens online. You do not hold the dollars in your hand: through a broker, on a platform such as MT5 on your computer or phone, you trade on the price going up or down.

  • Currencies are always quoted in pairs, for example the euro against the US dollar, written EUR/USD.
  • If EUR/USD rises from 1.1000 to 1.1050, the euro has become stronger against the dollar.
  • Not only currencies: Gold is traded too, as XAU/USD.
  • Pips, lots and spread come in the next lessons.

The honest truth about risk

Forex is not a job and not a get-rich-quick scheme. Price differences make losses just as easily as profits. Many regulated brokers state on their own websites that most of their retail clients' accounts lose money. The usual reason is trading without learning, without a plan and with too much risk. Anyone who promises "guaranteed profit" or "fixed daily income" is showing the biggest red flag (read about signal scams).

Is forex trading legal?

The rules are different in every country. In India, for example, the Reserve Bank of India allows residents to trade forex only through authorised persons and on recognised exchanges, for permitted purposes. Before you start anything, check the official rules of your own country. Our guide to forex trading in Pakistan and India explains the details.

The most common beginner mistake

Watching two videos, or taking someone's signal, and putting real money in on the first day. The right path is: learn first, practise on a demo account, make your own plan, then start small. This whole series follows that path.

Questions

Forex trading kya hai?

Forex trading ek currency ko doosri currency ke muqable mein khareedna ya bechna hai, taa ke qeemat badalne se faida ho. Misal: dollar Rs 280 par khareed kar Rs 285 par bechna. Forex market banks, companies aur traders ka duniya bhar ka currency market hai jo hafte mein 5 din, 24 ghante khula rehta hai.

What is forex trading in simple words?

Forex trading is buying or selling one currency against another to profit from a change in its price, for example buying US dollars at Rs 280 and selling them at Rs 285.

How does the forex market work?

Banks, companies, institutions and individual traders exchange currencies through a worldwide computer network, 24 hours a day, five days a week. Prices move with demand and supply.

How big is the forex market?

According to the BIS Triennial Survey, average daily forex turnover was about $9.6 trillion in April 2025, which makes it the largest financial market in the world.

Can you lose money in forex trading?

Yes. The same price change that gives one trader a profit gives another a loss. Many regulated brokers state that most of their retail clients lose money. Learn first and practise on a demo account.

Is forex trading legal in Pakistan and India?

The rules depend on the country. In India the RBI allows residents to trade forex only through authorised persons and recognised exchanges for permitted purposes. Always check your own country's official rules.

How do I start forex trading as a beginner?

Learn the basics in order (this free series), open a demo account, practise with a written plan and small risk, and only then think about a small real account.
