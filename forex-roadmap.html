Three rules for the whole journey

  • Do not skip steps. Strategies and indicators fail without the basics and risk control underneath.
  • Practice must be checked. Share your charts with your reasons; corrections are where learning happens.
  • Demo first, then small live. Real money only after a written plan and around 100 recorded practice trades.

Questions

Where should a beginner start learning forex?

Start with the basics: pips, lot size, spread, leverage, market timings and a free MT5 demo account. Then learn price action before any indicator or strategy.

How long does it take to learn forex?

With daily study, the basics and price action take about one to two months, and checked practice of around 100 trades takes another two months. Consistency usually takes longer.

Can I learn forex for free in Urdu or Hindi?

Yes. All the courses, guides and practice batches on this roadmap are free, in Urdu and Hindi, with charts explained step by step.

When should I start trading with real money?

After about 100 recorded practice trades with your charts checked, a written plan and a small account where one loss cannot hurt you.
