Forex trading in Pakistan and India: rules, brokers, scams, Gold and hours

Before you open any trading account, know the rules where you live. Here is what the regulators in Pakistan and India have said, in plain words, with links to the official notices, plus the scams to avoid and the hours that matter.

Pakistan: what the regulators say

  • State Bank of Pakistan, 18 May 2022 (EPD Circular Letter No. 08 of 2022): banks must stop customer payments to offshore forex, margin and CFD trading websites and apps, through any payment channel, under the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act 1947.
  • SECP, 9 April 2025 (press release): warned the public about online trading platforms offering forex, commodities and other speculative products without regulatory approval in Pakistan.

In simple words: sending money from Pakistan to an offshore forex or CFD broker is not a permitted payment, and those platforms are not licensed in Pakistan. If something goes wrong, Pakistani regulators cannot help you get your money back.

The regulated route for trading Gold and other futures in Pakistan is the Pakistan Mercantile Exchange (PMEX), which is regulated by the SECP. Check PMEX's current contract list and use a PMEX-registered broker.

India: what the regulators say

  • RBI, 7 September 2022 (press release): residents may deal in forex only with authorised persons, for permitted purposes, on RBI-authorised electronic platforms or recognised exchanges (NSE, BSE, MSE). RBI keeps an Alert List of unauthorised forex platforms and says the list is not exhaustive.
  • RBI LRS rules (FAQ): the Liberalised Remittance Scheme does not allow remittances for trading foreign exchange abroad, or for margins and margin calls to overseas exchanges or counterparties.

In simple words: in India, currency trading is allowed on recognised exchanges through SEBI-registered brokers, in INR pairs (USD/INR, EUR/INR, GBP/INR, JPY/INR) and some cross pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY) as exchange-traded derivatives. Online margin trading with an overseas forex broker is not permitted and can lead to action under FEMA.

How to spot a forex scam

  • Guaranteed or fixed profit ("5% daily", "no loss"). Real trading has no guarantees.
  • "Give us your money, we trade for you" or account management by strangers.
  • Pressure to deposit more before you can withdraw, or withdrawal "fees" and "taxes".
  • Profit screenshots and Lamborghini lifestyle instead of rules and risk.
  • Fake copies of known names: a WhatsApp or Telegram account using a teacher's or broker's name and photo.
  • Recovery scams: someone promising to get back money you lost, for a fee.

Choosing a broker: what to check

We do not recommend or rank brokers. Whatever platform you look at, these checks protect you:

  • Your own country's rules first. In Pakistan the regulated route for futures is a PMEX-registered broker; in India, a SEBI-registered broker on a recognised exchange. See the sections above.
  • Regulation you can verify. Find the licence number on the broker's site, then look it up on the regulator's own website. Copy-cat "clone" firms use real firms' names.
  • Who holds your money. Regulated brokers keep client money in separate accounts. Cash collected by a local agent has no protection.
  • Withdraw before you deposit more. Test a small withdrawal early. A platform that delays withdrawals or asks for "tax" or "fees" first is a warning sign.
  • Total cost. Spread, commission and swap decide what each trade costs you.

The real risks of forex trading

Forex and Gold CFDs are leveraged: a small move against you can cost a large part of the account. Brokers regulated in the UK and EU must show what percentage of their retail clients lose money, and on most of them it is the majority.

  • Never trade with money you need for rent, fees or family expenses.
  • Risk a small fixed amount per trade, usually 1% of the account or less, with a stop loss on every trade.
  • No one can guarantee profit. "Fixed monthly return" is the most common scam line.

Gold trading in Pakistan

Gold is the most traded instrument among our students. Two very different things are called "Gold trading" in Pakistan:

  • Gold futures on PMEX: exchange-traded contracts through a PMEX broker, regulated by the SECP. Check PMEX's current contract list and margins.
  • XAUUSD with an offshore CFD broker: the international Gold price in dollars per ounce, the chart most YouTube analysis uses. Payments to offshore trading platforms fall under the SBP restriction described above.

The local bullion price per tola follows the same international price. One troy ounce is about 2.67 tola, so if Gold is $4,000 an ounce and $1 is 280 rupees, the base price is about 4,000 × 280 ÷ 2.67 ≈ Rs 420,000 per tola, before the local market's premium or discount. Market hours in Pakistan and India time

The forex market runs from about 2–3 AM Monday to 2–3 AM Saturday, Pakistan time. India (IST) is 30 minutes ahead of Pakistan (PKT).

  • Tokyo: about 5:00 AM–2:00 PM PKT (5:30 AM–2:30 PM IST).
  • London: about 12:00–9:00 PM PKT in summer, 1:00–10:00 PM in winter.
  • New York: about 5:00 PM–2:00 AM PKT in summer, 6:00 PM–3:00 AM in winter.
  • Best hours for most pairs and Gold: the London–New York overlap, about 5:00–9:00 PM PKT (5:30–9:30 PM IST) in summer and one hour later in winter.

Questions

Is forex trading legal in Pakistan?

Learning and practising forex is legal. But the State Bank of Pakistan has told banks to stop payments to offshore forex, margin and CFD platforms, and the SECP says such platforms are not licensed in Pakistan. The regulated route for futures such as Gold is PMEX.

Where can I learn forex trading in Urdu for free?

Our three free courses (Price Action Basic, Advance and Trading Strategies) are taught in Urdu / Hindi on YouTube, and every lecture has written notes and questions on this website.

Which forex broker is best in Pakistan?

We do not recommend brokers. In Pakistan the regulated route is a PMEX-registered broker; for any platform, verify the licence on the regulator's own website and test a small withdrawal first.

Can I trade Gold in Pakistan?

Yes, Gold futures are traded on PMEX through PMEX brokers, regulated by the SECP. XAUUSD with offshore CFD brokers falls under the SBP restriction on payments to offshore trading platforms.

Is forex trading legal in India?

Yes, on recognised Indian exchanges through SEBI-registered brokers, in INR pairs and a few permitted cross pairs. Trading with overseas online forex brokers and sending money abroad for it is not permitted under RBI rules.

Can I deposit with an international forex broker from Pakistan?

Under the SBP's 2022 circular, banks may not process payments to offshore forex, margin or CFD trading platforms. Check the current rules before sending money anywhere.

What is the RBI Alert List?

A list published by the Reserve Bank of India of entities that are not authorised to deal in forex or run forex trading platforms. RBI says a name missing from the list does not mean it is authorised.

How can I tell if a forex offer is a scam?

Guaranteed profit, someone offering to trade your money, pressure to deposit more, fees to withdraw and fake accounts using a known name are the main warning signs.

What is the best time to trade forex in Pakistan and India?

The London–New York overlap: about 5:00–9:00 PM Pakistan time (5:30–9:30 PM IST) in summer and one hour later in winter.
