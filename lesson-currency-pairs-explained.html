Lesson notes

What is a currency code? ISO 4217 in one minute

Every currency in the world has a three-letter code set by the global standard ISO 4217. Banks, airline tickets and MT5 all use the same codes so there is no confusion. The usual pattern: the first two letters are the country and the third is the currency: USD (United States + Dollar), PKR (Pakistan + Rupee), INR (India + Rupee), JPY (Japan + Yen), GBP (Great Britain + Pound), AUD, CAD and NZD.

It is a pattern, not a formula. CHF is the Swiss franc, from the Latin name Confoederatio Helvetica. EUR is shared by many European countries, so it is built from EU. Gold is XAU and silver XAG: X means not a country's currency, and Au is gold's chemical symbol from the Latin aurum. That is why gold in MT5 is XAUUSD.

Many brokers add a suffix such as EURUSDm, EURUSD.pro or EURUSD.ecn. The real pair is always the first six letters; the ending usually shows the account type. Right-click the symbol and open Specification to see the full details.

Base currency and quote currency: the petrol pump example

In forex you never buy one currency alone; you always buy one against another, so two codes are written together as a currency pair. The first is the base currency and the second is the quote currency.

A petrol pump board says: 1 litre = 250 rupees (for example). The litre is always one; the rupees show its price. A pair works the same way: in EURUSD 1.1011 the euro is the litre (always 1) and the dollar is the rupees. So the number is the price of one euro in dollars.

Common mistake: is 1.1011 the dollar's price?

No. In Pakistan we hear "the dollar is 276", so the mind thinks every number is the dollar's price. In forex language that is USDPKR 276: the dollar is first, so 276 is the dollar's price in rupees. In EURUSD 1.1011 the euro is first, so 1.1011 is the euro's price in dollars: one euro is about one dollar and ten cents, and with one dollar you will not get a full euro.

How to read any currency pair in 3 steps

  1. Say the first currency with the word one.
  2. Say equals.
  3. Say the screen number with the second currency.

EURUSD 1.1011: one euro equals 1.1011 dollars. GBPUSD 1.27: one pound equals 1.27 dollars. USDJPY 150: one dollar equals 150 yen (here the dollar is first, so the number is the dollar's price).

What Buy and Sell really mean

Buying EURUSD means buying euros and selling dollars; you expect the euro to strengthen against the dollar. Selling EURUSD means selling euros and buying dollars. The Buy or Sell decision is always about the base currency. If the chart goes up, the base currency is getting stronger: EURUSD from 1.1011 to 1.1050 means one euro now buys more dollars; down to 1.0980 means the euro got cheaper. Profit or loss also depends on spread and lot size, which we calculate in Module 2.

The rule: is the dollar on the left or the right?

When news says the dollar is getting stronger: in EURUSD the dollar is on the right (quote), so the pair goes down. In USDJPY the dollar is on the left (base), so the pair goes up. One news, two pairs, opposite directions. Always check where the dollar sits before you trade on dollar news.

Major, minor and exotic currency pairs

  • Major pairs: the US dollar against one of the strongest currencies. Usually seven: EURUSD, USDJPY, GBPUSD, USDCHF, AUDUSD, USDCAD, NZDUSD. The dollar is on one side of about 89% of all trades, and in the BIS 2025 survey EURUSD alone was about 21% of the market, USDJPY about 14% and GBPUSD about 8%. More buyers and sellers mean lower spreads.
  • Minor (cross) pairs: no dollar, but both currencies are big: EURGBP, EURJPY, GBPJPY, AUDCAD. Spreads are a bit higher, and GBPJPY is famous for fast moves ("the Beast").
  • Exotic pairs: one big currency plus one from an emerging or small economy: USDTRY, USDZAR, USDMXN. USDPKR and USDINR also belong here, but most brokers do not offer them. Few buyers and sellers mean wide spreads, sudden moves and higher swap.

Road analogy: majors are the motorway (heavy, smooth traffic, small toll), minors are city roads (rush and bumps), exotics are a village dirt road (few cars, potholes).

The family test

Memorise eight currencies: USD, EUR, JPY, GBP, CHF, AUD, CAD, NZD. If a pair has a currency outside these eight, it is exotic. If both are inside, check for the dollar: dollar = major, no dollar = minor. AUDUSD = major, EURJPY = minor, USDTRY = exotic. Brokers draw these lines slightly differently, and spreads change with the broker, account type and time of day.

Which currency pairs should a beginner trade?

Start with one or two majors such as EURUSD: low spread, smoother movement, and learning material everywhere. But no pair is safe: a low spread only means a lower cost. Before any trade, check the spread at your broker, check market activity, practise on demo, and decide your maximum loss first. In MT5, right-click Market Watch and open Symbols: most brokers keep Majors, Minors and Exotics in separate folders, where you can compare their spreads.

Questions

What is a currency pair in forex?

A currency pair shows the price of one currency in another. In EURUSD 1.1011, the euro is the base currency (always 1) and 1.1011 is its price in US dollars.

What is the difference between base and quote currency?

The base currency is written first and always counts as one unit; the quote currency is written second and shows the price of that one unit. Buy and Sell decisions are about the base currency.

What are major, minor and exotic pairs?

Majors pair the US dollar with a strong currency (EURUSD, USDJPY, GBPUSD, USDCHF, AUDUSD, USDCAD, NZDUSD). Minors or crosses have no dollar (EURJPY, GBPJPY). Exotics include a currency from a smaller or emerging economy (USDTRY, USDZAR) and usually have much wider spreads.

Which currency pair is best for beginners?

Most educators suggest starting with one or two major pairs such as EURUSD because spreads are low and price movement is smoother. No pair is risk-free, so practise on demo and set your maximum loss first.

Kya USDPKR forex mein trade hota hai?

USDPKR exotic family mein aata hai, lekin zyada tar forex brokers ise trade ke liye offer nahi karte. Apne broker ke Symbols mein check karein.
