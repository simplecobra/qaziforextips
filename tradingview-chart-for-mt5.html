What changes on your MT5 chart

LookTradingView Light & Dark

TradingView candle colours, background, price line and a clean chart with the grid off. Prefer your own F8 colours? Choose "Keep my own colours" and the toolbar, legend and boxes adapt to them.

LookOHLC legend, watermark, countdown

Symbol, timeframe, open, high, low, close and change at the top left, a soft symbol watermark in the centre and a candle close countdown next to price.

MovementDrag like TradingView

Drag left / right for smooth scrolling and up / down to move price freely: Auto scale turns off like TradingView. Click the price scale (or Shift+R) to snap back to Auto.

MovementZoom and space on the right

Ctrl + wheel zooms candles, Shift + wheel zooms price. Drag past the newest candle to add empty space on the right, up to 50% of the chart.

ToolbarSplit buttons and favourites

The big icon starts the tool, the small › arrow opens the list. Star any tool to put it on the main toolbar, just like TradingView favourites.

DrawingsOne click to select, drag to edit

Click once: blue handles and the drawing's own floating menu appear. Drag a handle to move one point, drag the body to move everything.

DrawingsTimeframe visibility

Show a drawing on all timeframes, on this timeframe and lower, or only here. Perfect for top-down analysis: mark an H4 zone and hide it on D1.

ExtrasMagnet, undo, hide, lock, screenshot

Weak or strong magnet to candle OHLC, undo, hide all, lock all, delete all and a chart screenshot saved without the toolbar.

The Long / Short Position tool (with real lot size)

Choose Long or Short Position and click your entry. The green zone is the target and the red zone is the stop, sized from the recent average range at your default reward:risk (2.0).

Drag the green Target label (or line) to move take profit, the red Stop label to move stop loss, the entry line for the entry and the centre Lot label to move the whole tool. The blue dots on both sides change the width.

Labels show price, pips and money. The centre shows the lot size and R:R, calculated from your risk (1% of balance by default, or a fixed amount) and your broker's contract size. If the lot is below your broker's minimum, the label warns you.

Install in 2 minutes

  1. Download the free zip and unzip it (right-click → Extract All).
  2. In MT5 open File → Open Data Folder → MQL5 → Indicators and paste QFT_TradingView_Chart.ex5 there.
  3. In the Navigator (Ctrl+N) right-click Indicators → Refresh, then drag QFT_TradingView_Chart onto a chart and press OK. No DLL is needed.
  4. Want every new chart in TradingView style? Right-click the chart → Templates → Save Template → name it default.

Put it on a chart only once. To update to a new version, first remove the old one: Ctrl+I → QFT TradingView Chart → Delete. It is an indicator, not an EA, so your Trade Assistant or any EA keeps working on the same chart.
