How to backtest in MT5 manually: QFT Replay Simulator tutorial

MetaTrader 5 has no bar-by-bar replay for manual traders. This tutorial shows how to backtest any strategy by hand inside your own MT5 or MT4 with QFT Replay Simulator: from installation to your first replay, a prop firm challenge and the report.

Tutorial notes

Quick answer

To backtest manually in MT5, install QFT Replay Simulator, download M1 or tick data for your pair in the Data Center, start a New Simulation from the date you want, then press Next Bar or Play and trade the chart as if it were live. The future stays hidden until you move forward. When you finish, export the report to see your win rate, drawdown, streaks and how much to risk per trade.

Step 1: Install the simulator in MT5 or MT4

  1. Download the zip from the simulator page and right-click it → Extract All.
  2. In MetaTrader: File → Open Data Folder → MQL5 (MT4: MQL4). Close MetaTrader.
  3. Copy folder 1 (the .ex5 or .ex4) into Experts and folder 2 (the .dll) into Libraries.
  4. Open MetaTrader, go to Tools → Options → Expert Advisors, tick Allow DLL imports and switch on Algo Trading.
  5. Drag the simulator onto any chart, for example XAUUSD H1.

The free demo has every feature unlocked: 5 trades in total on the last 31 days of data. For the full version you send your account number on WhatsApp and receive a licence key.

Step 2: Download data in the Data Center

Choose the symbol and the dates. M1 candles download fast and are enough for most swing and intraday testing; ticks are slower but exact, which matters for scalping and tight stops. Days you already downloaded are skipped, and you can also import years of M1 data from a CSV file. The data comes from your own broker, so spreads and symbols match your live account.

Step 3: Start a new simulation

Pick the start date, balance, leverage and commission. Since version 3.05 you can add up to 3 extra pairs that replay on the same second, for example Gold with the Dollar Index (DXY) to practise SMT divergence, or EURUSD with GBPUSD. Under every pair the window shows whether its data is ready.

Step 5: Practise a prop firm challenge

Press Prop Firm at any moment of the replay and choose 2-Step Phase 1 (10% / 5% / 10%), Phase 2, 1-Step, Instant funding or your firm's own numbers. The dashboard checks the profit target, daily loss and maximum loss on every tick, and fails you the way a real firm would. Rewind with Prev Bar and trade that day again.

Step 6: Read the backtest report

Export an HTML or CSV report with net profit, win rate, profit factor, maximum drawdown, expectancy and total R, an equity curve, a trading journal and your losing streaks. The money management section suggests a risk per trade from half Kelly and a 2,000-run Monte Carlo test. Questions

How do I backtest manually in MT5?

MT5's own Strategy Tester is built for EAs. For manual backtesting install a replay tool such as QFT Replay Simulator, download data for your pair, start a simulation from a past date and trade it candle by candle with Next Bar or Play.

Is there bar replay in MT5 like TradingView?

Not built in. QFT Replay Simulator adds bar replay inside MT5 and MT4 on your own charts, with every timeframe moving together.

Is QFT Replay Simulator a Soft4FX or FX Replay alternative?

Yes. It runs inside your own MetaTrader like Soft4FX, and adds a prop firm challenge mode, lot size from your $ risk and a report with a money management plan. See the comparison table.

Should I use M1 data or ticks?

M1 data is faster to download and fine for most strategies. Use ticks for scalping or very tight stop losses, where the order of prices inside a candle matters.

Can I backtest more than one pair at the same time?

Yes. Version 3.05 replays your main symbol and up to 3 extra pairs on the same second, useful for SMT divergence and correlation.

Is a good backtest a guarantee of profit?

No. A backtest is practice and evidence, not a promise. Test enough trades, then forward-test on a demo before using real money.
