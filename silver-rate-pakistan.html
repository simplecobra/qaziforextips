How the silver rate in Pakistan is calculated

Like gold, silver in Pakistan follows the international silver price in US dollars per ounce (XAGUSD) and the dollar rate (USD/PKR). This page uses that formula, so every number can be checked:

Silver price per tola = Silver price per ounce ($) × USD/PKR ÷ 31.1035 × 11.6638
925 sterling = 999 price × 0.925 · 1 kg = price per gram × 1,000

Silver moves much faster than gold in percentage terms. A 2–3% move in one day is normal, because silver is both a precious metal and an industrial metal used in solar panels and electronics.

Market rate vs sarafa rate

The rate here is the market rate, worked out live from the world price and the dollar. The sarafa (bullion market) rate you hear in the news is set by local dealers and is usually a little higher. Jewellers then add making charges for silver jewellery, and the price they pay you for old silver is lower than the selling price. Use this page to know the fair value of the metal itself.

Zakat nisab 2026 in Pakistan: 52.5 tola silver in rupees

Nisab is the minimum amount of wealth on which zakat becomes due. It is measured either by silver: 52.5 tola (612.36 grams) or by gold: 7.5 tola (87.48 grams). Because silver is much cheaper, the silver nisab is a far lower amount, and many people use it for cash and mixed savings. At today's market rate (14 November 2026), 52.5 tola of silver = Rs 264,915 and 7.5 tola of 24K gold = Rs 3,014,048.

Silver rate in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and other cities

The base silver rate is the same in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar and Quetta. Differences between cities come only from local dealer premiums and each shop's buy/sell spread.

Frequently asked questions

What is the silver rate in Pakistan today?

On 14 November 2026 pure (999) silver in Pakistan is Rs 5,046 per tola, Rs 4,326 per 10 grams and Rs 432,638 per kilogram, based on $48.65 per ounce and USD/PKR 276.60.

How is the silver price per tola calculated?

Silver price per tola = international silver price per ounce (USD) × USD/PKR ÷ 31.1035 × 11.6638. For sterling silver (925) multiply the result by 0.925.

Why is the sarafa silver rate different?

This page shows the market rate from the international price and the live dollar rate. Local bullion dealers add their own premium, and jewellers add making charges, so shop prices are usually higher.

What is the difference between 999 and 925 silver?

999 (fine) silver is 99.9% pure and is sold as bars and coins. 925 (sterling) silver is 92.5% silver mixed with copper for strength and is common in jewellery.

Is the silver rate the same in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad?

The base rate is the same across Pakistan. Small differences come from local dealer premiums and shop spreads.

How often is this silver rate updated?

Every 30 minutes on trading days, Monday to Friday. On weekends the last Friday rate stays on the page.

What is the nisab amount in Pakistan today?

The silver nisab is 52.5 tola (612.36 grams) of silver and the gold nisab is 7.5 tola (87.48 grams) of gold. Their value in rupees at today's market rate is shown in the nisab section above, and changes with the silver and gold price.
