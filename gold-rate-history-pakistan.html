Gold price per tola from 1947 to 2026

Key moments in gold's history

  • 1947–1971: the world price was fixed at about $35 an ounce under the Bretton Woods system, so the tola rate in Pakistan moved mainly with the rupee (Rs 3.31, then Rs 4.76 from 1955).
  • August 1971: the US ended the dollar's convertibility into gold and the price was set free.
  • January 1980: the first big peak, a monthly average of about $675 an ounce, after high inflation and the oil shocks.
  • July 1999: the lowest level in about 20 years, near $256 an ounce, about Rs 4,960 a tola in Pakistan.
  • September 2011: a new high near $1,772 after the 2008 crisis, about Rs 58,158 a tola.
  • August 2020: the COVID-19 high near $1,969; with the rupee at Rs 160+, the tola passed Rs 1 lakh.
  • 2022–2023: the rupee's biggest fall (dollar above Rs 300) pushed the tola rate up much faster than the world price, which rose only modestly.
  • 2024–2026: central-bank buying, rate cuts and global tension lifted gold above $3,000 and then $4,000 an ounce. The highest daily reference in our data was $5,539 on 29 January 2026; the highest tola price was Rs 581,299.

What Rs 1 lakh of gold in 2000 is worth today

Rs 100,000 (1 lakh) put into 24K gold at the 2000 average price (about Rs 5,523 a tola) would buy about 18.1 tola. At 23 November 2026's rate that gold is worth about Rs 6,817,985. The same Rs 1 lakh kept in dollars would be worth about Rs 523,247 today. This is a look back, not a forecast: gold also had long flat and falling periods (1980–2001, 2012–2018), and past prices do not tell what it will do next.

Why gold keeps rising in rupees

A tola in Pakistan is priced from the world gold price in dollars. So the local rate rises when gold rises abroad (inflation, interest-rate cuts, wars, central banks buying), and when the rupee falls. Since 2000 gold rose about 13.0 times in dollars while the dollar rose about 5.2 times in rupees; together that is about 68 times. How is the historical tola price calculated?

Tola price = gold price in US dollars per ounce × USD/PKR ÷ 31.1035 × 11.6638. Before March 2024 we use the monthly world price and the monthly average dollar rate (yearly before 1957), so older figures are estimates. 22K is the 24K price × 22/24.

Why is the sarafa bazaar rate different from this page?

Sarafa associations add a local premium, duties and dealer margins, and they update at their own times. Jewellery also adds making charges. Use this page for history and trends; check your jeweller for a buying price.

How much has gold gone up since 2000 in Pakistan?

From about Rs 5,523 per tola (2000 average) to Rs 376,531 on 23 November 2026, about 68 times. Two things pushed it: gold rose about 13.0 times in dollars and the dollar rose about 5.2 times in rupees. Past prices do not tell what gold will do next.
