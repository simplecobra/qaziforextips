Gold price per tola from 1947 to 2026

Gold in Pakistan has gone from about Rs 43 a tola in 1947 to Rs 368,468 in 2026, about 8,552 times. Two engines drive it: the world gold price in dollars, and the dollar rate in rupees (see the USD to PKR history). When both rise together, the tola rate jumps.

Key moments in gold's history

  • 1947–1971: the world price was fixed at about $35 an ounce under the Bretton Woods system, so the tola rate in Pakistan moved mainly with the rupee (Rs 3.31, then Rs 4.76 from 1955).
  • August 1971: the US ended the dollar's convertibility into gold and the price was set free.
  • January 1980: the first big peak, a monthly average of about $675 an ounce, after high inflation and the oil shocks.
  • July 1999: the lowest level in about 20 years, near $256 an ounce, about Rs 4,752 a tola in Pakistan.
  • September 2011: a new high near $1,772 after the 2008 crisis, about Rs 57,373 a tola.
  • August 2020: the COVID-19 high near $1,969; with the rupee at Rs 160+, the tola passed Rs 1 lakh.
  • 2022–2023: the rupee's biggest fall (dollar above Rs 300) pushed the tola rate up much faster than the world price, which rose only modestly.
  • 2024–2026: central-bank buying, rate cuts and global tension lifted gold above $3,000 and then $4,000 an ounce. The highest daily reference in our data was $5,539 on 29 January 2026; the highest tola price was Rs 581,299.

What Rs 1 lakh of gold in 2000 is worth today

Rs 100,000 (1 lakh) put into 24K gold at the 2000 average price (about Rs 5,614 a tola) would buy about 17.8 tola. At 20 November 2026's rate that gold is worth about Rs 6,563,473. The same Rs 1 lakh kept in dollars would be worth about Rs 515,843 today. This is a look back, not a forecast: gold also had long flat and falling periods (1980–2001, 2012–2018), and past prices do not tell what it will do next.

Why gold keeps rising in rupees

A tola in Pakistan is priced from the world gold price in dollars. So the local rate rises when gold rises abroad (inflation, interest-rate cuts, wars, central banks buying), and when the rupee falls. Since 2000 gold rose about 12.7 times in dollars while the dollar rose about 5.2 times in rupees; together that is about 66 times. Traders follow the same driver on charts as XAUUSD: see the gold trading guide and today's gold rate in Pakistan.

What was the gold rate in Pakistan in 1947?

About Rs 43 per tola of 24K gold, by our estimate: the world price was fixed at $35 an ounce and one US dollar was Rs 3.31. Local bazaar prices of that time could differ.

What was the 1 tola gold price in Pakistan in 2000, 2010 and 2020?

Yearly averages, estimated from the world gold price and the dollar rate: about Rs 5,614 in 2000, Rs 39,124 in 2010 and Rs 107,436 in 2020 for 24K gold per tola.

What is the highest gold rate in Pakistan so far?

In our daily data the highest 24K reference price was Rs 581,299 per tola on 29 January 2026, when gold was about $5,539 an ounce. Sarafa bazaar rates on the same day can be different.

How is the historical tola price calculated?

Tola price = gold price in US dollars per ounce × USD/PKR ÷ 31.1035 × 11.6638. Before March 2024 we use the monthly world price and the yearly average dollar rate, so older figures are estimates. 22K is the 24K price × 22/24.

Why is the sarafa bazaar rate different from this page?
