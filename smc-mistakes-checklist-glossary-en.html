Master checklist: before every trade

  1. Bias: which way is H4 and H1 structure? What is the invalidation level?
  2. DOL: which liquidity is the next target?
  3. Location: buys in discount, sells in premium?
  4. POI: score of 8 or more?
  5. Time: in a killzone, with no high-impact news in the next 30 minutes?
  6. Confirmation: a sweep, CHoCH or MSS with displacement, and an FVG?
  7. Risk: 1% or less, lot calculated, RR at least 1:2, within today's limit?
  8. Journal: plan and screenshot ready?

If any answer is "no", there is no trade. Not trading is also a position.

Daily schedule (Pakistan time, winter)

TimeTask
10:00 amTop-down (15 minutes): bias, DOL, POI, plan; mark the midnight open and Asian range
12:00 to 15:00London killzone: open the chart only on alerts
17:00 to 20:00New York killzone: check news times in advance
22:00Journal and a note for tomorrow
SundayWeekly review and the week's news

In summer (March to November) every time is one hour earlier. In New York time: the midnight open is 00:00, London 2 to 5 am, New York 7 to 10 am.

The whole course in one paragraph

Take bias from the higher timeframe, see which liquidity price is heading for, find a strong POI in the bias direction on the right side of discount or premium, wait for price to arrive in a killzone, confirm with a sweep and CHoCH, enter with 1% risk and at least 1:2 RR, take profit at liquidity, and journal every trade. Everything else is detail on these points.

What next?

  1. Write your trading plan on one page: model, timeframes, session, risk, daily limit.
  2. Complete 100 backtest trades.
  3. Demo, then small live, as in lesson 7.3.
  4. Share your journal and questions in the group every week; learning never stops.
RememberRemember: SMC is not magic and not a guarantee. It is an organised way to read a chart. Your profit depends on how patiently you take only the best setups and how strictly you follow your risk rules.

Spot it on the chart: practice

This is a real Gold chart (Dukascopy data, Pakistan time). Study it and pick an answer. After you answer, the fully marked chart and the explanation appear.

At 06:00 on 12 Oct a trader bought at 4,211.6 with the SL at 4,198.0. Price had rallied from 4,105.1. Which common mistake is this?

The 50% of the 4,105.1 to 4,216.9 move is 4,161.0. 4,211.6 is far above it: an expensive spot (premium). Price broke the SL, dropped into discount at 4,150.5 and rose from there: waiting for discount was better. An SL was set and it was not news time.

    Questions

    What is the SMC master checklist?

    Eight questions before every trade: bias, draw on liquidity, discount or premium, POI score, time, confirmation, risk and journal.

    What are the most common SMC mistakes?

    Trading lower timeframes without higher timeframe bias, treating wicks as breaks, buying in premium, trading quiet hours or news, and breaking risk rules.

    Does SMC guarantee profit?

    No. It is an organised way to read a chart; results depend on patience, taking only the best setups and following risk rules.
