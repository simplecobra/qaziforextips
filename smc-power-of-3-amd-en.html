Power of 3 (AMD)

Every daily candle tells a story: first price gathers near the open, then a false move traps traders, and finally the real move arrives. This is the Power of 3, or AMD: accumulation, manipulation and distribution. In this lesson you will learn to read it in New York and Pakistan time and trade from it.

What the Power of 3 means

The Power of 3 (PO3) says any period (a day, a week or a session) usually unfolds in three parts:

  1. A: Accumulation: price stays in a tight range around the open. Large players build positions quietly and liquidity builds on both sides.
  2. M: Manipulation: price makes a fast move against the real direction, sweeps one edge of the range and traps traders on the wrong side. This is also called the Judas swing.
  3. D: Distribution: the big move in the real direction, which makes most of the day's range. Large players take profit here.

It is the same story you saw in lesson 4.1: Asia (A), the London sweep (M), and the real move into New York (D).

AMD inside the daily candle

Look at a bullish daily candle: after the open, price first goes down (forming the lower wick), then rallies and closes near the high. So a bullish day's low often forms early, below the open. On a bearish day price first goes up (the upper wick), then falls and closes near the low. A bearish day's high often forms early, above the open.

RememberA simple rule: with a bullish bias, look for buys below the open (cheap). With a bearish bias, look for sells above the open (expensive). It is the premium and discount rule (lesson 3.6), applied to the daily open.

The midnight open

ICT treats New York midnight (00:00) as the day's "true open". In Pakistan this is:

  • Winter: 10:00 am
  • Summer: 9:00 am

Draw this level on the chart every day. Most brokers' daily open (server time) is different, so mark the midnight open with its own line.

The AMD map (winter, Pakistan time)

PartTime (PKT)What happensWhat you do
A: Accumulation06:00 to 10:00 (Asia)Range, liquidity on both sidesMark the range, PDH, PDL and the midnight open
M: Manipulation10:00 to 15:00 (mostly London)A false move against the open, an Asian edge sweptWait for a CHoCH after the sweep
D: Distribution15:00 to 22:00 (late London and New York)The big move in the real directionEnter from a POI, target the next liquidity

These times are a guide, not clock hands. Sometimes the manipulation comes at the New York open, and some days show no clean AMD at all (range days or big news days).

Trading AMD step by step

  1. Bias: from H4 and the daily, decide whether you expect a bullish or bearish candle today.
  2. Midnight open: draw the line and mark the Asian high and low.
  3. Wait for manipulation: with a bullish bias, price moves below the midnight open and sweeps the Asian low or a clear SSL.
  4. Confirmation: a CHoCH and displacement on M5 or M15 after the sweep.
  5. Entry: from the FVG or order block of the displacement, below the open (in discount).
  6. Stop loss: below the manipulation low. If the bias is right, this should be the day's low.
  7. Target: for the distribution, the Asian high, PDH or the next BSL.

For a bearish day, everything is reversed: sell above the open, stop above the manipulation high.

Weekly Power of 3

The same story plays out over a week. In a bullish week the weekly low often forms on Monday or Tuesday (manipulation), with the real move from Wednesday to Friday. In a bearish week the high often forms early. So in a bullish week, Tuesday's deep discount is often the best buying opportunity. This is a tendency, not a law.

Gold example

A Thursday in winter. Daily and H4 bias bullish.

  • 10:00 PKT: midnight open 4,124. Asian range 4,119 to 4,129.
  • 11:00 to 13:00: price fell below the midnight open and swept the Asian low (4,119) at 4,112. Retail sold, thinking support broke. This is the manipulation.
  • 13:15: an M15 CHoCH, the 4,121 high broke and an FVG formed from 4,114 to 4,118.
  • Entry: 4,117 (below the 4,124 open, in discount), stop 4,109, risk $8.
  • Distribution: in New York price reached 4,152. Half profit at the Asian high 4,129 (1:1.5) and stop to break-even; the rest at PDH 4,148 ($31, about 1:3.9).
  • The daily candle: open 4,124, low 4,112, high 4,152, close 4,146. A textbook bullish Power of 3.

Real Gold chart example

Gold M15 on 27 Oct 2026 (data: Dukascopy, bid price; Pakistan time). The New York midnight open was 09:00 in Pakistan that day (summer time):

  • A, accumulation: a range of 4,003.6 to 4,021.4 from 05:00 to 09:00. Midnight open 4,017.3.
  • M, manipulation: after 09:30 price moved below the open, broke the Asian low and reached 3,989.0 at 13:45. Anyone selling below the open was trapped.
  • D, distribution: after 15:30 the real move went up: 4,064.5 by 21:00.

The New York daily candle: open 4,017.3, low 3,989.0 (early in the day), close near 4,060. A textbook bullish Power of 3.

Common mistakes

Common mistakeTrading with the manipulation, thinking it is the real move. Selling below the open with a bullish bias usually means walking into the trap.
Common mistakeAMD without a bias. If you do not know whether the day is bullish or bearish, you cannot tell which move is false.
Common mistakeUsing the broker's server open as the midnight open. Many brokers start the day at a different hour; draw the midnight open separately.
Common mistakeForcing every day into AMD. Range days and big news days often do not form the pattern.

Summary

  • Power of 3: accumulation (range), manipulation (false move and sweep), distribution (real move).
  • A bullish day's low often forms early below the open; a bearish day's high above it.
  • The New York midnight open is 10 am in Pakistan in winter and 9 am in summer.
  • With a bullish bias buy below the open, with a bearish bias sell above it, with confirmation.
